ℹABOUT.HTM — sources & disclaimer
      

    

      
THE SUPREME CHEETO v2.0 — a satirical dashboard.

      
Every figure comes from a linked public source and is shown without alteration. Truth Social posts are reproduced verbatim; nothing is invented, and no quote is ever attributed to anyone who did not say it. The jokes live in the framing, not the data. Not affiliated with anyone.

      
Data freshness

      
Sources

      
Refreshed every 15 minutes by a scheduled job. If a source can't be reached, the previous value is kept and marked stale rather than replaced with a guess.


      
🔒 Your data
        

          
Reading this site needs no account and sets no tracking cookies.
          There are no analytics and no advertising trackers — the sponsor slots are plain
          links that don't report back.

          
If you sign in with Google or Discord, we store only what the site needs:
          your email address, a handle and display name, an avatar URL if you set one, plus
          whatever you choose to post — chat messages, statuses, bulletin posts, reactions,
          predictions and your friends list.

          
That data is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and isn't used for anything
          beyond running the site. It lives in a Supabase database; sign-in is handled by Google
          or Discord, who never pass us your password.

          
Deleting your account removes all of it immediately — the button is in
          My Profile. There's no grace period and no backup to restore from, so it really is gone.

          
Some things stay in your browser only and never
          reach a server at all: your window layout, theme, household size, visit counter and
          debt guesses. Clearing site data resets those.

        

      


      
⚖ Terms, briefly
        

          
This is satire and commentary on a public figure, built on
          publicly available data that's linked at every figure. Quotes are reproduced verbatim
          and never invented. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to anyone mentioned.

          
Be civil in chat and on the board. Harassment, threats, slurs and spam get you
          removed. Moderation is enforced by the database, not by argument.

          
Provided as-is, for entertainment. Don't make
          financial or voting decisions based on a cartoon gauge named after a snack.
